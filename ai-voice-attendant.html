How It Works

From ring to record
in under 30 seconds.

01
Your line forwards to FlowDesk Pro

You forward your existing business number to your dedicated FlowDesk Pro line. Setup takes under 30 minutes. No hardware. No IT department. Just a call forward from your phone carrier settings.

02
AI answers in your brand voice

Every call is answered within 2 rings by your AI Voice Attendant, greeting callers with your business name. It speaks naturally — no robotic menus, no hold music. Fully bilingual English and Spanish, automatic.

03
Caller intent and urgency captured

The AI identifies whether the caller needs service, scheduling, support, billing, or general information — then collects their name, phone number, reason for calling, and urgency level through natural conversation.

04
Structured lead record created instantly

A timestamped, urgency-classified lead record appears in your real-time dashboard within seconds of the call ending. No data entry. No sorting voicemails. Just organized, actionable information.

05
Your team gets an instant alert

High-urgency calls trigger immediate SMS and email alerts to your team. You know about hot leads within 30 seconds of the call ending — even at 2am on a Sunday. Your staff responds with full information, not a voicemail.
